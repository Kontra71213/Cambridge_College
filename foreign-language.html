Why Learn Japanese

  • Japan is among the world's top three economies with deep ties to India across automotive, electronics, pharmaceuticals and IT.
  • MEXT scholarship fully funds degrees in Japan — tuition, accommodation and a monthly stipend for qualified students.
  • JLPT certification is globally recognised and enhances employment prospects in Japanese MNCs operating in India.
  • Japanese N3 and above significantly strengthens MEXT scholarship applications.
  • Growing tourism, cultural and business interest in Japan is increasing demand for Japanese speakers.

Frequently Asked Questions - Japanese Language Classes in Chandigarh

Is Japanese hard to learn for Indian students?

Japanese can be learned step by step with regular practice and proper guidance.

What is the JLPT and why does it matter?

JLPT is the Japanese Language Proficiency Test and is recognised for study and career opportunities.

Can I study in Japan with JLPT N4?

N4 is a good starting point, but university requirements can vary.

What is the MEXT scholarship?

MEXT is a Japanese government scholarship for eligible international students.

Are online Japanese classes available?

Yes, online classes are available.
